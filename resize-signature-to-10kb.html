How to resize your signature to 10KB

Many exam portals reject a signature that is over 10 KB or the wrong pixel size. This tool fixes both at once: it scales your scanned or photographed signature to 140×60 pixels, places it on a clean white background, and compresses it to just under 10 KB. Upload your signature above, keep the Signature 10KB preset selected, and click Resize & compress.

Get a clean result at 10KB

10 KB is a tight limit, so the clearer your source, the better:

  • Sign on plain white paper with a dark black pen.
  • Crop tightly around the signature before uploading — remove empty space.
  • Avoid shadows and coloured or patterned backgrounds.
