How to resize your signature to 20KB

When a form allows up to 20 KB, you can keep a sharper signature than at 10 KB. This tool scales your scanned signature to 140×60 pixels, adds a clean white background, and keeps the file under 20 KB. The Signature 20KB preset is already selected — just upload and click Resize & compress.

10KB or 20KB — which should I use?

Always follow what your form says. If it allows a range (e.g. “10 KB to 20 KB”), 20 KB gives a clearer signature. If it says “maximum 10 KB”, use resize signature to 10KB instead.

Tips for a clean signature

  • Sign on white paper with a dark pen, then crop tightly around it.
  • Scan or photograph in good, even light — avoid shadows.
  • Keep the background plain white; the tool will whiten it further.

Private by design

Your signature never leaves your browser — there is no upload and nothing is stored.

Questions

Can I set a size between 10 and 20 KB?

Yes — type any number into the KB box (for example 15), then click Resize & compress.

What about the pixel size?

This defaults to 140×60 px, the common requirement. You can change width and height in the exact-dimensions panel if your form asks for something different.
