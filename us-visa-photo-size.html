US visa photo requirements

The US Department of State requires a digital photo that is square, 600×600 to 1200×1200 pixels, and under 240 KB in JPEG format. This tool is preset to 600×600 px under 240 KB — upload your photo and click Resize & compress.

How to get a good result

  • Use a front-facing photo with a plain light or white background.
  • Keep your whole head and the top of your shoulders in frame.
  • The tool crops to a perfect square — center your face before uploading for the best fit.

Note: this tool handles the digital size and format. Always check the official composition rules (expression, lighting, background) on the Department of State website before submitting.

Private — nothing is uploaded

Your photo is processed entirely in your browser and never sent to a server, so your passport photo stays yours.

Questions

My photo came out cropped oddly.

The US photo must be square, so a tall or wide photo gets cropped to 600×600. Crop your photo to roughly square first, keeping your face centered, then upload.

Is 600×600 enough?

Yes — 600×600 is the minimum accepted size and the most reliable. The file will be kept under 240 KB.
